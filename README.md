# weather_tracker
**Tracking weather data in Ed R. Levin Park, in Milpitas CA**
What this overall project is about is to find out if the temperatures at Ed R. Levin Park are too hot for camping during the month of August. The data that it uses is from the Open Meteo API to fetch temperature data. The main outcome was two CSV files that show the historical weather of the park and the forecast, and also compares 5 years of historical August temperatures with a 7-day forecast for Ed R. Levin Park.
- Made by a seventh grader, summer of 2026
